In plain words

NISAR measures how far the ground moved along its slanted line of sight, compared with a reference area set to zero. Moving away usually means sinking, but sideways motion can also contribute. Four months is a short record; published studies cover years. [The finding appears once the interferograms are processed and reviewed.]
