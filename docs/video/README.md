# Demo video (29 s)

`tanaw-demo-30s.mp4`: 1920×1080, 30 fps, 29.0 s, with a background music track.

**SI-generated (made with AI tools), video and audio.** It carries a visible watermark saying so on every frame, and the file's metadata (title and comment) states that both the video and the music were made with AI tools. It is assembled from
screenshots of the real running app on real NASA-ISRO NISAR data; no images were generated by an AI model. No NASA logos.

How it was made (scripts in `src/`, written with AI assistance):
1. `capture.js`, `zoom.js`, `radar.js`: Playwright captures of the app (backend on :8000, frontend on :3000, real data
   package installed), including the map zoomed to the Bulacan-coast fishponds where the recurring water is.
2. `scenes.js`: composes each 1920×1080 scene in HTML (screenshot, caption, watermark) and renders it to PNG.
3. `build.sh`: ffmpeg turns the scenes into the video (slow zoom on stills, fades).
4. `audio.sh`: **the music is original and SI-generated**: synthesized entirely by AI-written code with ffmpeg (a soft chord pad, filtered-noise rain, and radar "pings" timed to the four NISAR pass frames). It uses no samples, recordings or copyrighted material.

Every number in the captions comes from the app and `docs/NISAR_RUNBOOK.md`.
